Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you give us directly:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense records you enter, including amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to let you log in, record your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
